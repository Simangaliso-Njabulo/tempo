# Keyboard and VoiceOver

## Keyboard

- Tab moves through the skip link, date controls, the timeline, tray actions, and sheet controls.
- Enter or Space opens a focused block.
- Escape closes a sheet and returns focus to the control that opened it.
- Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside an open sheet.
- The **Add block** sheet has labeled title, start time, and duration fields.
- The detail sheet offers **Move** and **Duration** alternatives to dragging.

## VoiceOver

- A block announces its type, title, start time, duration, and state.
- The carried badge is spoken as “Carried over.”
- The reflow preview is a live status announcement.
- Drag has a non-drag alternative: **Place** in the tray and **Move** in the detail sheet.
- Reduce Motion removes the FLIP and ghost animation; the drop still works.

## Touch

- Every interactive target is at least 44 × 44 pt, with 8 px between adjacent targets.
- No important action depends on a horizontal swipe.
- No action depends on a long press.
